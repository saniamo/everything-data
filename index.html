02 / PROGRAMME ANALYSIS

Participation data and entrepreneur outcomes

I analysed routine programme data to examine whether consistent participation across programme activities was associated with stronger revenue and employment outcomes among entrepreneurs.

I linked participation records with entrepreneur-level outcome data and used Excel and SPSS to examine patterns across levels of engagement, sectors, regions and business types. The analysis found that entrepreneurs who were consistently engaged throughout the programme reported stronger business outcomes than those whose participation was more irregular.

What changed

The findings highlighted the value of monitoring participation and engagement throughout the programme, rather than focusing only on whether entrepreneurs completed the programme. Programme managers could identify declining engagement earlier and follow up with entrepreneurs while there was still time to support their participation.
