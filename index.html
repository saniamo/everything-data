About

I started my career in data analysis before moving into monitoring, evaluation and learning. At the African Management Institute, I progressed from Data Analyst to MERL Officer and then MERL Manager.

My work has covered theory of change and measurement frameworks, indicator development, survey and data collection tools, data quality, analysis, dashboards, reporting and programme learning. I've worked across programmes in Kenya, Rwanda and other African countries, including entrepreneurship, livelihoods, youth employment and financial inclusion.
