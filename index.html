Selected work

Four programmes, four different problems.

Kenya

LIF Double Down

Measurement system design for a programme concentrating extra support on high-potential businesses.

Context

The programme wanted to concentrate additional support on the businesses most likely to create jobs, but had no agreed way to identify them.

My role

Led the M&E design: theory of change, measurement approach, indicator framework and baseline design.

What I did

Worked backwards from the intended outcomes to map the causal pathways, made the assumptions explicit, and connected each pathway to indicators and data sources.

Result

A theory of change, results framework, indicator framework and baseline design the programme used to structure its targeting approach.

Theory of ChangeLogframe & IPTTSurvey designData protection
Rwanda

BRD AFIRR / HATANA

Reporting dashboard rebuild for a development bank's SME finance programme.

Context

A Rwandan development bank's SME finance programme produced plenty of data, but stakeholders couldn't see progress against targets without manual work each reporting round.

My role

Rebuilt the M&E reporting dashboard used by World Bank and BRD stakeholders.

What I did

Restructured how SME performance indicators were defined, collected and consolidated, then rebuilt the reporting layer.

Result

Teams could see progress against targets during implementation, spot issues earlier, and query the data directly.

Multi-country

FIRST+II

Impact analysis behind an annual donor report, built from self-reported business data.

Context

The programme needed to report changes in business performance using participant-reported revenue and employment data.

My role

Designed and ran the analysis for the annual impact report for CapPlus and the Mastercard Foundation.

What I did

Matched programme participants across reporting periods and calculated compound annual growth rates, applying interquartile-range outlier treatment before estimating growth.

Result

The analysis contributed to the programme's annual impact reporting.

Global

Financial inclusion fund

A standalone theory of change built for BFA Global.

Context

A financial inclusion programme had activities and indicators, but no explicit account of how the intervention was expected to produce change.

My role

Built the theory of change as a standalone deliverable for BFA Global.

What I did

Mapped the causal pathways and surfaced the assumptions underneath them, reviewing existing impact evidence, including findings from randomised-trial research, to inform the logic.

Result

A theory of change document setting out the causal pathways, assumptions, and what would need to be measured during implementation.

About

I am a monitoring, evaluation and learning professional with 4.5 years of experience in programme data, measurement, evaluation and reporting.

At African Management Institute, I progressed from Data Analyst to MERL Officer and then MERL Manager. My work has covered MERL framework development, theory of change, indicator design, survey and data collection tools, data quality, quantitative and qualitative analysis, dashboards, donor reporting and learning.

I have worked across programmes funded by organisations including the Mastercard Foundation, Argidius Foundation, GIZ, USAID, AGRA and the Dutch Good Growth Fund, with experience spanning Kenya, Rwanda and multi-country portfolios.

My background in data analysis continues to shape how I approach MERL. I work from the underlying dataset through to the final report or dashboard, and pay close attention to how definitions, collection methods and data quality affect the conclusions that can be drawn.
