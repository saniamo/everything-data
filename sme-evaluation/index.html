Question 2

Was the programme associated with better outcomes?

To separate the programme from everything else going on, each participant was matched to a similar business that did not take part, on revenue, practices, workers, owner gender, business age, country and sector at the start. The estimate is how much more participants changed than their matches. The answer stays close to the main estimate under every check below, and the two groups were growing at the same pace before the programme began.

Method in brief

Surveys at the start and 12 months later, for participants and a comparison group drawn from businesses that applied for a later round. Each participant was matched to the most similar comparison business. The estimate compares how much each group changed, controlling for starting differences, with ranges that allow for uneven spread in the data. Revenue gains are calculated on a log scale so that a few very large businesses do not dominate. Analysis in Python. Interviews coded in a shared framework, with a second coder reviewing a sample of transcripts.

Limits: matching only accounts for what the surveys measured, revenue and profit are self-reported, and 12 months may be too short to see job creation in full.
